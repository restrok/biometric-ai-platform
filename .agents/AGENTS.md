# Workspace Engineering & Coaching Rules

## 🏃 Coaching & Gear Adaptations
- **Respect User Clothing Preferences**: Do not recommend running in long pants or tights if the user has a stored preference for running in shorts/bare legs in cold weather.
- **Provide Alternative Thermal Compensations**: When running in cold weather ($<15^\circ\text{C}$) in shorts, recommend alternative physiological strategies to protect the leg musculature:
  1. *Indoor dynamic warmups* (to raise muscle temperature to $\approx 39^\circ\text{C}$ before stepping outside).
  2. *Mild warming/heat creams* (to stimulate local vasodilation in quadriceps/calves).
  3. *Active recovery intervals* (treading/jogging in Zone 1 during recoveries instead of standing/walking) to prevent rapid cooling of the muscle.
  4. *Immediate post-run cover-up* (putting on warm sweatpants immediately after the run ends).
